SMART TRAFFIC - standalone app (no server needed)

Everything is built into the app: the 4 cameras and their videos, live traffic
numbers, alerts, analytics and reports. It works offline. Internet is only used
to draw the street map tiles.

Sign in:  free@gmail.com   /   freeaccount

1) In your project folder (flutter_application_1), paste this folder's contents
   over it: lib, assets, tool, test, pubspec.yaml. Keep your android/ ios/ web/.
2) In a terminal inside the project:
      flutter pub get
      flutter run -d chrome          (quick preview on PC)
3) Build the APK for your phone:
      dart run tool/patch_android.dart
      flutter build apk --release
   File: build\app\outputs\flutter-apk\app-release.apk
   Copy it to your phone and install it.

What persists on the phone: sign-in, settings, password change, camera names,
alerts and their status, saved snapshots.
